Technical Story

Engineering cases

Problem → investigation → root cause → corrective action → validation.

01 — Spindle Overload Fault

ProblemCNC stopped with EX1041 TOOL 102 OVERLOAD FAULT.
InvestigationVerified incoming three-phase supply at approximately 463–464 VAC, then traced the spindle-drive power path.
Root CauseDamaged, cracked and heat-affected spindle cable found in the cable-carrier area.
ValidationAlarm cleared after correction and the machine completed a hardwood cutting test under load.

02 — Automatic Tool Changer Collision Recovery

ProblemCarousel collision damaged a tool-holder clamp and affected alignment relative to the spindle.
CorrectionMechanical alignment was restored, then the OEM-prescribed recovery/calibration procedure was followed.
ValidationTool-change positioning and operation were checked before return to normal use.
LessonMechanical correction alone was insufficient; control/calibration relationships also had to be restored.

03 — Conveyor / Material-Handling Alignment

ProblemMaterial handling gradually tracked toward one side during operation.
InvestigationInspected frame geometry, roller relationship, guides, spacing and material path.
CorrectionUsed laser-assisted alignment and spacing/guide corrections to restore geometry.
ResultRecurring tracking issue was corrected and consistent material movement restored.
