Case 01

Spindle overload: from alarm to physical root cause

Initial condition

FaultThe CNC stopped with EX1041 TOOL 102 OVERLOAD FAULT.
First questionWas the overload coming from the incoming power, the spindle drive, the motor path or a physical wiring problem?

Troubleshooting sequence

Electrical verificationIncoming three-phase supply was checked at approximately 463–464 VAC, confirming the supply was present and balanced enough to continue fault isolation.
Safe inspectionThe spindle-drive circuit was isolated before deeper inspection of the VFD-to-spindle power path and machine cabling.

Root cause & validation

Root causeA damaged, cracked and heat-affected spindle cable was identified in the cable-carrier area.
ValidationAfter the cable was replaced, the overload alarm cleared on restart and the machine completed a hardwood production test cycle under load.
The visible alarm described the condition; the physical cable inspection identified the failure.

Case 02

Automatic tool changer collision recovery

ProblemA carousel collision damaged a tool-holder clamp and affected alignment relative to the spindle.
Mechanical recoveryPositioning and alignment were inspected and corrected before controls-side recovery.
CalibrationThe carousel was recalibrated using the OEM-prescribed tool-changer recovery procedure.
ValidationTool-change positioning and operation were verified before the machine returned to normal use.
Public portfolio note: specific maintenance-mode bits, parameter values and detailed OEM service sequences are intentionally omitted.

Case 03

Conveyor material-handling tracking & alignment

Recurring issueMaterial handling gradually tracked toward one side after repeated operation.
InspectionFrame/roller relationship, spacing, guides and material path were checked rather than treating it as a one-time belt adjustment.
CorrectionLaser-assisted alignment, spacing correction and guide positioning were used to restore the required geometry.
ResultConsistent material movement was restored and the recurring drift was corrected.
